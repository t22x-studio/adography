About Ado

Drawn by a lifelong desire to empower others through song, Ado chose her stage name from the supporting role in traditional Japanese Kyogen theater—a subtle nod to supporting her listeners from behind the scenes. Growing up on Vocaloid culture and inspired by Hatsune Miku, she began her journey in 2017 as a net-native utaite, recording vocals inside a DIY soundproofed closet booth.

In late 2020, her major debut single "Usseewa" ignited a cultural phenomenon, shattering domestic charts and turning her overnight into the voice of a modern generation.

Known for her astonishing vocal gymnastics—effortlessly switching between delicate whispers, intricate range leaps, and signature growls (ganari)—she made global history in 2022 as the singing voice of Uta in ONE PIECE FILM RED. Her hit "New Genesis" (Shin Jidai) became the first Japanese song in history to reach No. 1 on the Apple Music Global Top 100 Chart, while tracks from the same soundtrack album swept the top positions of the Billboard Japan Hot 100.

From her anthem "KIRA", which backed the Japan National Football Team for the 2026 World Cup campaign, to her sold-out global arena tours, Ado continues to reign at the forefront of modern J-pop without revealing her face, proving that pure vocal mastery needs no identity.

"Adography" is a curated digital archive dedicated to cataloging her music videos, live stage performances, and Vocaloid covers.
