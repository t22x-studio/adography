👉 [https://adography1024.vercel.app](https://adography1024.vercel.app)

**Adography** is an unofficial, fan-supported curated Ado video archive built for a distraction-free experience and a way to discover your new favorite Ado song 💙

Here you can explore her official discography including Kyogen, Zanmu, Ado's Utattemita Album, Ado's Best Adobum, or listen through our custom-curated **[Mood Playlists](https://t22x-studio.github.io/adography/#playlists)**!

---

### ✨ Features
- 🎬 **Curated Video Archive**: Official MVs, stadium live concerts (*e.g. Shinzou*, *Profile of Mona Lisa*, *Mars*), and Vocaloid covers.
- ⚡ **Continuous Web Player**: Persistent background playback with queue management and auto-advance.
- 🌙 **OLED Dark Mode & Theatre Mode**: Built for immersion with zero distractions.
- 🔍 **Instant Multi-Language Search**: Fast lookup across English, Romaji, and Japanese titles.

*Disclaimer: Adography is a non-commercial, fan-made project. All music and video streams are embedded directly via official YouTube and Niconico players.*
